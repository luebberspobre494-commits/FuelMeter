## Description draft

Track fuel use and service for every car you own, without an account and without ads.

Log a fill-up in a few taps: date, odometer, liters and price. FuelMeter works out consumption between full tanks, cost per kilometer, monthly spend and how far you have driven. Two charts show the trend so you can spot a sudden jump in consumption.

The Service tab keeps oil changes, inspections and insurance renewals together, with a reminder on the day they are due. The Garage tab holds several vehicles so a second car does not mix its numbers with the first.

Everything is stored on your device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no login and no third-party tracking.

## 4.3 (spam) answers

1. **What does the app do that the others do not?** It combines fuel consumption maths (only between full tanks, so partial fills do not distort the average) with per-vehicle service reminders and a garage that keeps several cars separate, entirely offline.
2. **Is it a template or a reskin?** No. The UI, data model and statistics are written for this app.
3. **Does it duplicate one of your other apps?** No other app in the account covers fuel or vehicle maintenance.
4. **Is the content unique?** Yes, all text and the icon are original.

## Privacy

No data collection. `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is `false`. No usage description keys are required: notifications are optional and requested only when a reminder is saved.
